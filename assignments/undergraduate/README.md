# Undergraduate Track

The undergraduate track uses the shared readings and lectures in the main course schedule but has its own assessment sequence.

## Assessment

| Assessment | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Discussion Questions | 40% |
| Final Project Draft | 20% |
| Final Project | 40% |

## Discussion Questions

Undergraduate students complete ten Discussion Questions during the semester.

The Discussion Questions progressively develop the conceptual material needed for the Final Project.

Responses:

- must address the assigned prompt;
- should demonstrate understanding of the relevant readings and lectures;
- should use clear reasoning;
- should be no more than 500 words unless otherwise specified; and
- are submitted through GitHub Discussions.

See [Using GitHub Discussions](../../DISCUSSIONS.md).

### Discussion Question Evaluation

Discussion Questions are evaluated on:

- quality of reasoning and argumentation;
- understanding of the intelligence-analysis and ontology material;
- clarity and organization;
- appropriate engagement with course sources; and
- creativity and insight.

## Final Project Draft

The [Final Project Draft](project-draft.md) is submitted in Week 9.

The draft establishes:

- the intelligence-analysis problem;
- project scope;
- competency questions;
- preliminary ontology work; and
- sources or ontology content that may be reused.

## Final Project

The [Final Project](../final-project.md) is due in Week 15.

A small ontology that is carefully designed, clearly documented, and well justified is preferable to a large ontology assembled without sufficient attention to modeling quality.
